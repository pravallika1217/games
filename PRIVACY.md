# Privacy Policy: ALIBI: Daily Detective

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

ALIBI: Daily Detective ("the app") is a daily mystery puzzle game. This policy explains what
happens to your information when you play.

## The short version

**The app does not collect, store or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no ads,
no analytics and no tracking.

## What stays on your phone

The app saves a few things **only on your own phone**, so the game works:

- The detective name you type in (for example "Inspector Priya").
- Your game progress: cases solved, stars, streak and today's result.
- Your sound setting, and the cases downloaded for offline play.

This information never leaves your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

## Downloading new cases

To get each day's new case, the app downloads small case files (the story and the puzzle)
from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). These downloads send no information about you.
Like any website, GitHub's servers can see your device's IP address when a file is downloaded;
see [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Sharing your result

If you tap **Share** on the result screen, you choose where to send the text (WhatsApp, for
example). The shared text contains only your stars, time and mistakes, never your personal data.
The app doesn't see or store what you share.

## Children

The app is made for players aged 13 and over. It doesn't knowingly collect any information from
anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
