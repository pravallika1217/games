## Full description (max 4000 characters)

🕵️ You're the detective. Every day, a new case lands on your desk.

📞 The Commissioner calls. A body at a wedding. A trophy gone in the rain. Poison on a live cooking show. You have one day to crack it.

🔍 HOW IT WORKS
• Examine the scene: check the body, the broken lock, the cut rope. What really happened?
• Meet everyone who was there. One of them did it.
• Follow the clues. Each clue leads to the next, and each one rules people out.
• Question the suspects. Show them your evidence and catch the liar: GOTCHA!
• Make the arrest. You only get two warrants.

⭐ DAILY PUZZLE, LIKE WORDLE
• One new case every day, the same for everyone
• About 5 minutes to solve
• Earn up to 3 stars, keep your streak alive, and share your result (no spoilers!)
• Climb the ranks from Rookie to Commissioner

🇮🇳 STORIES YOU KNOW
Sangeet nights, gully cricket, office Diwali parties, college fests, overnight trains. Real places, funny suspects, clever twists.

No sign-up. No ads. Just you, the clues, and a liar to catch.

Can you crack today's case?
